About Server

Luminaria SMP is a premium survival experience designed to transform Minecraft into something far beyond ordinary survival. We focus on building a welcoming community where peaceful gameplay, teamwork, exploration, and competitive PvP events coexist.


Whether you're an experienced builder, explorer, redstone engineer, warrior, or simply looking for friends to play with, Luminaria offers countless adventures through custom systems, kingdoms, events, economies, and future content updates inspired by RPG games.

📜 Server Rules

ORDINANCE NO. 001

The Luminaria SMP Peace and Conduct Act of 2025

Peaceful Gameplay

Respect every player. Griefing, stealing, random PvP, harassment and toxic behavior are strictly prohibited.

Land Protection

Use /selection and /claim to protect your land. Building near another player's claim without permission is not allowed.

Cheating

Hacked clients, x-ray, duplication glitches, exploits and unfair advantages are prohibited.

Staff Authority

Staff members enforce server rules fairly. Their decisions may be reviewed by the Owner.
