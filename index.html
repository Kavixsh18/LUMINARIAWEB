More than just another Minecraft server.

Luminaria SMP is a custom Minecraft Survival server dedicated to providing players with a unique and long-lasting multiplayer experience. Instead of vanilla survival, the server introduces enhanced gameplay systems, custom content, challenging bosses, unique progression, exciting PvP events, nations, player economy, and community-focused activities.

📜 Peace and Conduct Act

ORDINANCE NO. 001

"The Luminaria SMP Peace and Conduct Act of 2025"

AN ACT Establishing the Code of Conduct, Player Responsibilities, and Prohibited Acts within the Luminaria SMP Server, and for Other Purposes.

SECTION 1. Title

This Act shall be known as the "Luminaria SMP Peace and Conduct Act of 2025."

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy

It is hereby declared the policy of Luminaria SMP to maintain a peaceful, cooperative, respectful, and enjoyable environment for all players.

The preservation of peace, fairness, safety, creativity, and mutual respect shall always remain the guiding principles of the server.

SECTION 3. Scope and Application

These rules apply equally to

  • Players
  • Helpers
  • Moderators
  • Administrators
  • Content Creators
  • Every individual connected to Luminaria SMP.

SECTION 4. Prohibition of Griefing

Destroying, stealing, burning, exploding, or intentionally damaging another player's property without permission is strictly prohibited.

Respect every player's builds, farms, storage, and creativity.

SECTION 5. Player Killing

Random Player versus Player combat is not permitted unless both parties agree, or the activity takes place inside an official PvP Event.

Trapping, spawn killing, or repeatedly targeting another player is prohibited.

SECTION 6 — Standards of Respectful Conduct

(a) All players shall maintain respectful communication at all times.

(b) Harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, excessive toxicity, or any form of abusive behavior is strictly prohibited. Every player is expected to contribute to a welcoming and friendly environment for everyone.

SECTION 7 — Prohibition of Cheating and Exploits

The use of hacked clients, x-ray texture packs, auto-clickers, macros, duplication glitches, cheating software, or any unauthorized modification that provides an unfair gameplay advantage is prohibited.

SECTION 8 — Land Claim and Building Regulations

(a) Players shall clearly indicate and protect their land or nation by using /selection and /claim.

(b) No player shall construct buildings or establish settlements within close proximity of another player's territory without permission from the owner.

(c) Farms, storage systems, trading halls, and any player-made structures shall not be used without the owner's explicit consent.

SECTION 9 — Administrative Authority

(a) Server administrators are vested with the authority to enforce all provisions of this Ordinance.

(b) Administrators may issue verbal warnings, temporary punishments, suspensions, or permanent removal from the server whenever necessary to preserve peace and order.

(c) Decisions made by Staff Members and Helpers shall be subject to the approval of the Server Owner whenever required.

SECTION 10 — Effectivity

This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its publication and official announcement within the Luminaria SMP community. Every player joining the server is deemed to have accepted and agreed to abide by these rules.
