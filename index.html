Elemental Visions

The gods choose those worthy of wielding elemental power. Every player begins without a Vision. By obtaining a Vision Gem, destiny will decide which element you receive.

Anemo

Masters of the wind, specializing in mobility, crowd control, and agile combat.

Hydro

Manipulate water to heal allies, control the battlefield, and adapt to any fight.

Pyro

Command blazing flames to overwhelm enemies with explosive attacks.

Geo

Harness the strength of earth to create shields, defensive structures, and powerful strikes.

Electro

Unleash lightning-fast attacks capable of devastating multiple enemies.

How to Unlock a Vision

1 Obtain a Vision Gem.
2 Hold the Vision Gem.
3 Execute /vision unlock.
4 Receive one random Vision: Anemo, Hydro, Pyro, Geo or Electro.
